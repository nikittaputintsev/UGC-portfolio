02 — ABOUT

Hey, I’m
Nikitta.

Washington-based, and genuinely into the products I film — mostly AI tools.

I’ve been making videos since I was a kid. The products changed; the part I like didn’t.

Stop-motion animations and dumb little movies, then a business I tried to start that didn’t work. Somewhere in the middle of that it clicked: I didn’t want to run the company, I wanted to make the videos for it. So now I do that.

I’m obsessed with AI tools, which is why most of my work lands in tech, health and productivity. I write my own scripts, send multiple hooks per brief, and I won’t film a product I wouldn’t use — which probably costs me some work, and is the entire point.
